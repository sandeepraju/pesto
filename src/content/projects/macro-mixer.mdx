## What it is

A protein-shake scale app. Sit your shaker on the kitchen scale, scoop ingredients one at a time, and the app tallies macros as you go.

## Why it exists

I'd been eyeballing my shakes for years. Then I started actually weighing them and realised my "20g protein" scoop was 14g. The app makes the scale visible and the targets explicit.

## The single best feature

Save a shake as a template, then drag a slider to scale every ingredient proportionally up or down. The post-workout 40g and the breakfast 20g are the same recipe at different volumes; the slider lets you express that without re-keying anything.
