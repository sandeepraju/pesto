## What it is

An iOS app for people who make pizza at home and have ever burned a stretch-and-fold by forgetting the timer. It calculates dough ratios from your flour weight, hydration target, and proofing schedule, then schedules notifications for each step.

## Why I built it

I'd been keeping a notebook of my dough experiments for a year and realised the limiting factor wasn't the recipe — it was *remembering when to do the next thing*. The app is essentially a notebook with a timer attached.

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't have a recipe library. You bring your own.
- It doesn't have an Apple Watch app. (Maybe one day.)
- It doesn't sync to iCloud. The dough is local; so is the data.

## The technique it taught me

Building the calculator forced me to actually understand baker's percentages. Now I can riff on dough ratios in my head, which has affected my real-world cooking more than any individual recipe ever did.
