## What it is

A combinatorial cooking guide. Three columns: pasta shape, sauce, topping. Pick one from each and the app generates a numbered cooking sequence with timings.

## Why combinatorial

Most weeknight pasta is a remix of things you already know how to do. The art is in the combination, not in any individual technique. Pasta Planner treats the choice as a small grid problem and lets you riff.

## What's in the data

About sixty pasta shapes, twenty sauces, and thirty toppings. Not every combination is good — *gnocchi with cream sauce and chilli flakes* is fine, *bucatini with pesto and tomato* is not — but the app doesn't gatekeep. You're an adult.
